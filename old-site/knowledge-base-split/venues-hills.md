### The Lalit Grand Palace, Srinagar

**Location:** SRINAGAR · DAL LAKE

**About:** The Lalit Grand Palace Srinagar is a majestic masterpiece of Himalayan heritage, originally built in 1910 as a royal residence for the Maharaja Pratap Singh. Overlooking the serene Dal Lake, this iconic palace offers palace rooms, suites, and exclusive cottages as a regal sanctuary for a destination wedding. The property provides an unparalleled fairytale setting, boasting sprawling, manicured historic lawns under century-old Chinar trees. Indoors, the elegant Darbar Hall offers sophisticated, old-world charm for intimate rituals. Celebrated for its breathtaking valley views, Kashmiri hospitality, and timeless royal architecture, this palace offers an extraordinarily grand and romantic stage for a heritage wedding celebration.
