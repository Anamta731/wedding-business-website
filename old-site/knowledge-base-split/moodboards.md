# Moodboards & Wedding Themes

## About Moodboards
Vows & Vedas offers a curated library of wedding moodboards covering Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, and Wedding ceremonies. Each moodboard has a name, color palette, tags, and a description of the theme and atmosphere. Couples can use these to explore styles and communicate their vision to the design team.
