# [ITINERARY] The 2-Day 4-Function Wedding Flow
CATEGORY: itinerary
TAGS: itinerary, wedding flow, 2-day, 4-function, mehendi, sangeet, haldi, choorah, pheras, reception, standard, blueprint, functions, ceremony

A proven blueprint for an immersive, emotionally resonant wedding weekend — designed to build anticipation, honour tradition, and culminate in a breathtaking main ceremony. Every detail is flexible — this is your starting point, not your script.

FUNCTION 1 — Day 1 · Afternoon
Welcome Lunch & Vibrant Mehendi
Henna artistry, live music, and an atmosphere of joyful anticipation.

FUNCTION 2 — Day 1 · Evening
High-Energy Sangeet & Afterparty
Choreographed performances, live artists, and a celebratory afterparty that sets the energy soaring.

FUNCTION 3 — Day 2 · Morning
Intimate Haldi & Choorah Ceremony
Turmeric blessings, family traditions, and quiet moments of connection.

FUNCTION 4 — Day 2 · Sunset
Main Pheras & Formal Reception
Sacred vows under a canopy of stars, followed by a world-class reception dinner.
