# Planning Packages

## Package Tiers

**name:** Full Planning Package
**budget_tier:** ₹3–8 Lacs
**description:** Complete wedding planning from scratch. Suitable for weddings requiring end-to-end coordination without the scale of a destination event.
**includes:** Venue sourcing and selection | All vendor negotiations | Contract management | Detailed design conceptualisation | Guest management support | Multiple planning meetings | Site visits | Post-wedding vendor settlements

**name:** Full Luxury / Destination Planning Package
**budget_tier:** ₹8–15 Lacs
**description:** The same comprehensive planning as the Full Planning package, elevated for destination weddings and luxury-scale events. The inclusions remain identical — what changes is the scale of execution, the level of detailing and personalisation, and the ratio of staff and team members to guests.
**includes:** Venue sourcing and selection | All vendor negotiations | Contract management | Detailed design conceptualisation | Guest management support | Multiple planning meetings | Site visits | Post-wedding vendor settlements

**note:** These are planning fees only and do not include venue costs, vendor costs, decor, catering, or other event expenses. For a full wedding budget estimate, share your city, guest count, and vision and the team can provide a tailored breakdown.
